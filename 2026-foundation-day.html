Understanding research and extension

What they mean, and how they contribute to academic development, innovation and community engagement.

Definitions

Creating knowledge, then putting it to work

Research

A systematic process of investigating questions or problems to generate knowledge, evidence, technologies or solutions.

  • Starts from a question or problem without a ready answer
  • Follows a method others can check and repeat
  • Produces findings, a dataset, a prototype, a model or a paper

In CCS: algorithms, AI/ML, HCI, networks, cybersecurity, data science, ICT for development

Extension

The process of sharing and applying knowledge, skills and technologies to benefit communities, schools, industries, government offices and other stakeholders.

  • Trainings, workshops and digital literacy programs
  • Technology transfer and system deployment
  • Consultancy and technical assistance to partners

In CCS: coding camps, ICT skills for teachers, online safety talks, systems for barangays

From the MSU-IIT Manual on Research and Extension

Support the Institute puts in writing

Get paid on a projectStudents as project staff

Bona fide MSU-IIT students may be engaged as project staff on job-order status.

  • Research assistant: gather and analyze data, review literature, prepare progress and final reports, attend project meetings
  • Research enumerator / field interviewer: collect survey data, check forms before leaving the area, keep responses confidential
Manual, Sec. 6.2.4 (Project Staff)
Protected and creditedStudents in extension

The manual sets four safeguards for students on extension projects:

  • Official role, listed in the project team
  • May be credited as OJT or practicum, if your department approves
  • Voluntary, with your informed consent
  • Acknowledged by your department and college, and covered by a Special Order
Manual, Sec. 3.2.2.3
Open to studentsResearch Dissemination Award (RDA)

Undergraduate and graduate students presenting their thesis with the MSU-IIT byline can apply under Category B: registration fee support of up to USD 250, oral or poster.

  • Be bona fide and currently enrolled
  • Get recommendations from your adviser, chair and dean
  • Apply at least 2 months before the conference
  • Submit certificates and a short report within 1 month after
  1. You applyform RDA_001 + acceptance letter, full paper
  2. Dept. of Researchchecks and endorses
  3. VC for Researchrecommends approval
  4. Chancellorapproves; a Special Order is issued
  5. Present, then reportwithin 1 month

Incomplete applications are not processed. If you can't attend, the full award must be returned. The same paper can't be funded twice.

RDA guidelines ↗Manual, Sec. 9.2 · BOR Res. No. 46 s. 2019; No. 287 s. 2014
Faculty & staff onlyResearch Publication Award

Paid to faculty and staff for Scopus-indexed papers with the MSU-IIT byline (articles from July 20, 2026). Students can't claim it, but co-authoring with your adviser puts your work in these journals.

Publication Award guidelines ↗Manual, Sec. 9.3 · BOR Res. No. 4 s. 2020; EO No. 00020-2026. Proceedings: ₱30,000.

Sources: MSU-IIT Manual on Research and Extension (Sec. 3.2.2.3, Sec. 6.2.4 Project Staff, Sec. 9 Grants and Incentives); Office of Research Management pages on the RDA and Publication Award. Contact: ovcre.dr@g.msuiit.edu.ph · (063) 222-8630.
